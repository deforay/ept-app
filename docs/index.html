e-PT

A free and open-source Android app for external quality assessment (EQA) and proficiency testing in HIV testing programmes.

e-PT lets participating laboratories and testing sites receive proficiency testing shipments, record results for schemes such as DBS-EID, DTS HIV serology, HIV viral load, rapid HIV recency testing and COVID-19, and download their individual and summary reports.

The app is a client. It connects to the proficiency testing server operated by your own programme or organisation, using a server address you enter when you sign in.
